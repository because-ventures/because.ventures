About the company

Lola Olivia seeks to be the leader in providing undergarment solutions to young adults. They offer undergarments to a variety of young adults, no matter their gender identity. The mission is to uplift, empower and support our youth. They seek to modernize how young people shop for undergarments and provide transparency in the shopping process. We also endeavor to provide support and resources to young people, their parents and allies.
