About the company

EcoCart is on a mission to make commerce less harmful to the environment. Our platform calculates the carbon emitted from your business operations and offset it by partnering with certified carbon offsetting projects. It offers clients consumers the option to offset their carbon emissions at checkout.

EcoCart sources and vets the best sustainability projects and pairs them to your business. Analyze, offset, track, and communicate your ecological footprint with a sustainability platform backed by a robust network database.
